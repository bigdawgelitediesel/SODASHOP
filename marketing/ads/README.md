## Ad text

### Ad 1: Trailer
- **Primary text:** Rapid City's rock & roll soda trailer is on tour! 🎶 Handcrafted dirty sodas, custom energy drinks, flavored waters, and sweet treats, every one named after a classic hit. Check this week's stops and come find us. 🥤
- **Headline:** It's not just a soda. It's your soundtrack.
- **Description:** New stops around Rapid City every week.

### Ad 2: Fall drinks
- **Primary text:** Fall is on the setlist. 🍂 Spice Up Your Life (Dr Pepper, cinnamon vanilla, pumpkin spice, cream), Apple Bottom Sips, and Oops! I Bit It Again are pouring now, plus hot chocolates for chilly days. Find the Jukebox this week. 🎶
- **Headline:** Name that tune. Then sip it.
- **Description:** Fall seasonal menu through December.

### Ad 3: Jukey
- **Primary text:** Jukey's on tour! 🥤 Catch The Jukebox Soda Shop at parks, schools, games, and festivals around Rapid City and the Black Hills. Dirty sodas, energy drinks, and sweet treats. See where we'll be this week and come say hi! 👋
- **Headline:** See where Jukey is this week
- **Description:** Dirty sodas • Energy drinks • Sweet treats

## Targeting (suggested starting point)
- **Location:** Rapid City, SD + 25 miles (add Spearfish on weeks you're there).
- **Age:** 18–54.
- **Interests:** soft drinks, Dr Pepper, Mountain Dew, food trucks, coffee shops, local events.
- **Goal:** Traffic (to the tour page) or Awareness.
- **Budget:** $5–10/day per ad for a week, then keep the one with the cheapest link clicks.

## Editing
The images are built from the HTML in `source/`. To change text, edit the HTML and rerun `render.mjs`
(Playwright + Chromium), or ask Claude.
